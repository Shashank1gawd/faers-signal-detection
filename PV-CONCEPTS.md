# PV concepts behind this project

- **Pharmacovigilance (PV):** monitoring the safety of medicines after they are on the market.
- **FAERS:** the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System. Spontaneous reports from patients, clinicians and companies. Useful for hypotheses, not for incidence.
- **ICSR:** Individual Case Safety Report, the record of one patient's suspected adverse reaction.
- **Signal:** information suggesting a possible new or changed drug–event relationship that deserves investigation.
- **Disproportionality analysis:** compares how often an event is reported with one drug versus all other drugs.
- **2×2 table:** a = drug + event, b = drug, no event, c = other drugs + event, d = other drugs, no event.
- **PRR:** proportional reporting ratio = [a/(a+b)] / [c/(c+d)].
- **ROR:** reporting odds ratio = (a/b) / (c/d).
- **Evans criteria:** a ≥ 3, PRR ≥ 2, χ² ≥ 4.
- **IC / EBGM:** Bayesian-style measures used by WHO and FDA; approximated here.
- **Dechallenge / rechallenge:** stopping the drug (does the reaction resolve?) and restarting it (does it return?). Strong causality clues when documented.
- **Confounding by indication:** the disease being treated, not the drug, may explain the event.
- **MedDRA PT:** Preferred Term, the standard reaction term.
